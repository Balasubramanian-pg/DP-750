## Power Platform Integration with Azure Databricks

The Microsoft Power Platform empowers users to build custom applications, automate workflows, and analyze data with minimal coding. Integrating this suite with Azure Databricks bridges the gap between high-performance data engineering and agile business application development. This connection allows organizations to leverage governed, high-quality data from the Lakehouse directly within the tools business users rely on daily.

### The Azure Databricks Connector

The integration is facilitated by the **Azure Databricks connector**, which serves as a secure bridge between Power Platform services and your Unity Catalog-managed data. This connector enables:

*   **Power Apps:** To read from and write to Unity Catalog tables, allowing for the creation of data-driven business applications.
*   **Power Automate:** To trigger and orchestrate complex data workflows, including the execution of Databricks jobs or notebooks based on business events.

### Governance and Security

A critical advantage of this integration is the preservation of existing security policies. Because the connector interacts directly with Unity Catalog:

1.  **Centralized Control:** Access controls, row-level filters, and column masks defined in Databricks are enforced when Power Platform services access the data.
2.  **Secure Data Flow:** Business users can interact with sensitive data through approved applications without requiring direct access to the underlying storage or compute infrastructure.
3.  **Auditability:** All data interactions initiated from Power Platform are logged within the Databricks audit trail, ensuring compliance and visibility.

> [!NOTE]
> This integration transforms Azure Databricks from a backend engineering platform into a foundational data source for the entire organization. By empowering business users to build solutions on top of governed data, you reduce shadow IT risks while accelerating the delivery of actionable insights and automated processes.
