# Identify Azure Databricks Workloads

Azure Databricks supports a broad spectrum of data and AI initiatives, ranging from foundational data engineering to advanced large language model (LLM) development.

## Core Workload Areas

*   **Data Engineering**
    *   Facilitates collaboration between scientists and engineers on complex processing tasks.
    *   Leverages an integrated **Apache Spark** environment for big data processing within a data lakehouse architecture.
    *   Supports multi-language development using **Python, R, Scala, and SQL**.
    *   Enables end-to-end data exploration, visualization, and pipeline development.

*   **Machine Learning**
    *   Supports building, training, and deploying models at scale.
    *   Integrates **MLflow** for comprehensive lifecycle management, including experimentation, reproducibility, and deployment.
    *   Compatible with major frameworks such as **TensorFlow, PyTorch, and Scikit-learn**.

*   **SQL & Analytics**
    *   Designed for analysts who interact with data primarily through SQL.
    *   Utilizes **SQL warehouses** for high-performance querying.
    *   Provides a familiar interface with a SQL editor, dashboards, and automatic visualization tools.
    *   Ideal for ad-hoc queries and reporting on large datasets.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **SQL warehouses** are exclusively available on the **Premium tier**. Ensure your workspace is configured accordingly before attempting to provision these resources.
