## Deploy bundle with Databricks CLI

After your team configures a Declarative Automation Bundle, deploying it to Azure Databricks becomes your next responsibility. The **Databricks CLI** provides a direct path from your local development environment to production workspaces. You control when and where your pipelines, jobs, and other resources go live. Understanding this **deployment workflow** enables you to move code reliably across environments while maintaining consistency.

Note

This unit focuses on CLI-based deployment, which is the recommended approach for CI/CD automation. Declarative Automation Bundles can also be managed through the Azure Databricks workspace UI (Public Preview), useful for ad-hoc collaboration without installing the CLI. See [Collaborate on bundles in the workspace](/en-us/azure/databricks/dev-tools/bundles/workspace "/en-us/azure/databricks/dev-tools/bundles/workspace").
